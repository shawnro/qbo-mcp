# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 28, 2026

**Application:** SV Equipt MCP Connector ("the Application")
**Operated by:** Ops Group Holdings LLC, on behalf of its affiliated entities including SV Equipt LLC, RanchOps LLC, and FarmOps LLC ("we," "us," "our")

## 1. Overview

This Application is an internal tool used solely by Ops Group Holdings LLC and its affiliated entities to connect QuickBooks Online accounting data to internal automation and reporting tools. The Application is not distributed publicly, is not available on any app marketplace, and is not used by any third party outside of our organization.

## 2. Information We Access

The Application connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization process and may access the following categories of data within the authorized QuickBooks Online company:

- Customer, vendor, and account records
- Invoices, bills, payments, and other transactions
- Company information necessary for accounting operations

## 3. How We Use This Information

Data accessed through the Application is used exclusively for internal accounting, bookkeeping, and operational automation within our organization. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Access credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored securely and are not shared outside of our internal systems. We take reasonable measures to protect data accessed through the Application from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 5. Data Retention

Data retrieved through the Application is retained only as long as necessary for internal accounting and operational purposes, consistent with standard recordkeeping practices.

## 6. Third-Party Services

This Application interacts with QuickBooks Online, a service provided by Intuit Inc. Use of QuickBooks Online is also governed by Intuit's own privacy policy and terms of service.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application owner at Ops Group Holdings LLC.
